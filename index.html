Local-First Privacy Guarantee
  • 100% on-device processing. This application runs entirely within your web browser. Your classroom videos, audio recordings and generated transcripts are processed locally on your computer's hardware.

Privacy & Local Data Guarantee

1. Zero cloud data storage. This application runs entirely in your browser. All classroom video, audio recordings, transcripts and files stay on your device. There is no server behind it: ("the Owner") does not collect, stream, store or have any access to your recordings or student data.

2. Transcription on your device. Words and speakers are produced by models that run inside your browser on your own computer. Your audio is not sent to any transcription service, is never stored elsewhere, is never sold, and is never used to train any model. The one exception is the optional live draft while recording, which is off unless you turn it on: it uses your browser's own speech recognition (Google's in Chrome and Edge, Apple's in Safari) under that company's terms.

3. Local authorization. You are responsible for making sure that recording video or audio in your classroom follows your school district's media-consent and technology-use policies.
